### Midnight Bowling Last Strike

<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/2085419" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://nimclay.itch.io/midnight-bowling-last-strike">Midnight Bowling Last Strike by nimclay</a></iframe>

Midnight bowling is a first person horror adventure game, set in a bowling alley at night. The game includes a bowling pin monster, that is an active threat throughout the night. The first game was made poorly, as a quick project to give to my friends. However, it showed significant success in on itch, which I felt was undeserved.

The sequel was made to write the wrongs of the first game, containing more polish, less bugs, and signficantly more content. While this saw significantly less success than the first installment, it better represented my software development skills at the time (I made this during year 11/12 highschool). I have significantly improved since.

If you are to try one of my games to see my current skills, these games do not represent my full potential. Instead, try Rebirth of the Forsaken.
