# Greenbird Simulator

<iframe frameborder="0" src="https://itch.io/embed/1941391" width="552" height="167"><a href="https://nimclay.itch.io/greenbird-simulator">GreenBird Simulator by nimclay</a></iframe>

Greenbird simulator was my first large scale game project, that from a new save, can easily take 10+ hours to complete. It is a fast pace endless game, where the player completes quests for their children, otherwise they die. The player can find more species of eggs, to give a buffer before losing. Once all eggs die, a final stand occurs where the player fights an endless hoard of enemies whilst powered up, losing the game after dying.

## Unlockable Content
This game was my first exposure to adding extra unlockable content. This includes several skins, alternative levels, linear 3D platforming challenges, and more.

## Architecture
Since this was my first game, the architecture is poor, and as such the game is highly unoptimized. The state of the codebase gave me a great lesson on the importants of clean, scalable code, as it provided significant roadblocks when adding new features.

The full code, showing the shear volume of code required for such a project, is shown on my github. This is merely for archival purposes, and should not be taken as a demonstration of my skills.
