# Fitcast
Fitcast combines weather and clothing data to recommend the most comfortable outfit for the day. The app includes a high amount of customizability, improving the user experience. The app was developed in java, using a JavaFX frontend, a SQLite database, and JUnit tests. The app was developed over a semester as a group of six.
